## Controls, fairness and leakage assessment

- **Supervised controls:** ordinary supervised and `supervised_extra_updates` exist. The latter replays SSL window order and matches encoder/predictor update opportunities, with a reset optimizer and matched downstream schedule. Its head also trains during warmup. This is **step-budget matching**, not established FLOP, wall-time or energy equality. Define and measure the intended compute quantity before claiming compute-matched conclusions. Preserve per-phase steps/examples/time/RSS and attributed shared SSL costs; do not double-count shared SSL wall time.
- **Random-frozen:** implemented from the paired random initialization, with unchanged encoder/predictor and trained forecasting head. Frozen JEPA must reuse the same per-seed persisted SSL artifact as fine-tuned JEPA. Existing tests cover frozen-state invariance and checkpoint identity; no new V2 fit is asserted here.
- **TCN:** implemented causal left padding, monotone quantiles and the proposed 44,620-parameter/253-bin receptive field. It receives only the supplied 120-bin context plus padding, not 253 observed minutes. Keep the approved task/horizon/common support identical; record collapsed interval frequency.
- **Quantile-linear:** implemented full-context values with complete-support mask enforcement, zero initialization, seeded minibatch optimization, per-λ validation selection, smallest-λ ties and quantile rearrangement. It is stochastic despite zero initialization: **fit every approved seed**, not one forecast copied across seeds. Record λ, crossings and boundary selections.
- **Temporal isolation:** partition-local context/target/dependency containment and recomputed unique-raw-training normalization are implemented. Touching boundaries avoid support overlap but do not establish statistical independence. Any one-day embargo changes fitting support/normalization and requires an explicit loader/config amendment. Archived-product processing causality and actual delivery remain UNKNOWN; no operational claim follows from bin-end coordinates.
- **Selection fairness:** all paired arms must share the approved data subset, normalization, validation content and seed roster. Neural selection among 15 epochs and linear selection among 7/8 λ values have different search opportunities. Selected validation losses are **post-selection**, not unbiased generalization estimates. Alternate-loss jobs change training and checkpoint-selection objectives together; they compare bundled recipes. Deterministic classical replication must be declared and never counted as independent seed runs.
